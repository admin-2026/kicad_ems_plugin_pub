Solder mask material missing

This run includes the solder mask, but the coating has no thickness or no relative permittivity (Epsilon R, εr) — so there is nothing to mesh it with.

Three ways out

  1. Say what the mask is. Open Advanced > Materials > Solder mask and pick a coating, or Custom… and type εr and the loss tangent from your fabricator's datasheet. Save as… keeps it under a name of your own, on every board.
    Coatingεr (typ.)tan δ
    LPI solder mask3.2 – 4.00.02 – 0.03
    Dry-film solder mask3.8 – 4.20.03
    Polyimide coverlay3.4 – 3.60.008
  2. Let the board say it. Open File > Board Setup > Board Stackup > Physical Stackup, give the F.Mask / B.Mask rows a thickness (typically 0.01 – 0.04 mm) and an εr, press OK and save the board (Ctrl+S). The thickness has no other source — a picked material supplies εr and the loss tangent, never how thick the coating is.
  3. Leave the mask out. Untick Advanced > Materials > Simulate the solder mask coating. The board is then solved bare — which is how it starts out, and much the faster run.
Why it isn't defaulted: a trace's fringing field runs through whatever sits on top of it, so a coating with an invented εr moves the resonance by tens of MHz in a direction nobody chose — and a mask meshed at a guessed thickness is a different board from the one on the bench. The run stops instead of guessing.
