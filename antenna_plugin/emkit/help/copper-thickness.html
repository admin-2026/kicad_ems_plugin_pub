Copper thickness missing

A copper layer in the saved stackup has no thickness (or a zero thickness). The banner names the layer, e.g. F_Cu or In1_Cu.

How to fix it

  1. Open File > Board Setup > Board Stackup > Physical Stackup.
  2. Find the named copper row and enter its Thickness. If your fabricator's stackup sheet does not say otherwise, the standard foil weights are:
    Foil weightThickness
    ½ oz0.0175 mm
    1 oz (most common)0.035 mm
    2 oz0.07 mm
  3. Press OK and save the board (Ctrl+S).
Why it isn't defaulted: copper thickness feeds the conductor-loss model (skin effect) and the exact geometry of the foil in the FDTD grid. The plugin only simulates numbers you actually entered, so a missing value stops the run instead of being silently replaced.
